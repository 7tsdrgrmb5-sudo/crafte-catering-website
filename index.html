Frequently Asked

The questions clients actually ask us.

Tap any question for the quick answer. If your event is a little different, DM us and we’ll build the quote around it.

Does CraftE provide the alcohol?

Our standard service is client-supplied alcohol. Once your menu and guest count are set, we give you guidance on what to purchase and recommended quantities.

What does the $450 starting package include?

Our starting service is built around professional bartending, up to 5 hours of service, a customized drink menu, setup and breakdown, and the standard bar supplies listed in your quote. Final pricing depends on your event details.

Do you provide mixers, garnishes and bar supplies?

Standard mixers, garnishes and bar supplies included with your package are listed in your quote. Specialty cocktail-specific ingredients, premium garnishes, décor and other upgrades may be quoted separately.

Who provides the ice?

Clients generally provide sufficient ice. We’ll recommend an amount based on your guest count, service length and final drink menu so you aren’t guessing.

How many bartenders will my event need?

That depends on total guests, drinking guests, service style and how quickly drinks need to move. We recommend the staffing level in your quote rather than forcing every event into the same setup.

Are guests limited to the featured cocktails?

No. Your featured menu highlights the signature drinks, but our bartenders can also make standard mixed drinks when the necessary ingredients are available.

Can you serve beer and wine too?

Yes. If beer and wine are supplied for the event, our bartenders can serve them alongside your cocktail or mocktail menu.

Can you create a custom drink or mocktail menu?

Absolutely. We can tailor featured cocktails and mocktails to your theme, flavors and preferences, including fully alcohol-free events.

Do I need to have a built-in bar?

No. Portable bar setups can be part of the service. We’ll confirm the venue workspace, access and setup needs before the event so service can run smoothly.

How far do you travel?

We primarily serve Los Angeles County and Orange County, with the Inland Empire and surrounding Southern California areas available by quote. Longer travel may include an additional fee.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can for popular weekends and peak dates. Last-minute inquiries are still welcome and are accommodated based on availability; a short-notice fee may apply.

How do I secure my date?

A 30% booking retainer secures your event date, with the remaining balance due 24 hours before service. If CraftE changes the agreed pricing while your event is still at least 7 days away, you may decline the change and receive the retainer back. Urgent or unforeseen situations are reviewed case by case.

Why can two events with the same guest count cost differently?

Guest count is only one part of the quote. Service hours, drinking guests, staffing, travel, specialty ingredients, setup needs, décor and add-ons can all change the final price.

What if weather or an unexpected situation changes the plan?

Reach out as soon as possible. Depending on the situation, we can discuss an alternate setup, rescheduling or another reasonable solution based on the event and costs already committed.
