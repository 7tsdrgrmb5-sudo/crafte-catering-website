Frequently Asked

Before you book.

Does CraftE provide the alcohol?

Our standard service assumes the client provides alcohol. We’ll help you determine exactly what and how much to purchase based on your guest count and final menu.

Are guests limited to the featured cocktails?

No. Your featured menu highlights your signature drinks, but standard mixed drinks can also be made when the necessary ingredients are available.

Can you serve beer and wine?

Yes. If beer and wine are supplied for the event, our bartenders can serve them along with the cocktail menu.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, 6–12 months is ideal when possible. For smaller private events, 3–6 weeks is a good target. Short-notice bookings may be available and can include a last-minute fee.

How is my date secured?

Your date is officially reserved once the 30% non-refundable deposit is received. The remaining balance is due 24 hours before the event.

Can you do an alcohol-free event?

Absolutely. We can create a fully customized mocktail experience for teen parties, family events, corporate gatherings and alcohol-free celebrations.
